System Thesis

FXPM is a regime-aware strategy and trade-management selection engine. It is not trying to be a classical train/validation/test ML model. The optimizer finds local winners; live execution decides whether those winners are feasible under account, broker, margin, spread, sizing, and duplicate-position constraints.

Current Flow

Data

300k M5 Workflow

Latest 300,000 M5 bars are loaded. The oldest 50,000 are historical stress audit. The newest 250,000 are the active production universe.

Stage 1

Baseline Eligibility

Baseline strategies are kept or rejected by config-driven viability gates. The goal is candidate preservation, not final ranking.

Stage 2

Optimization

Expensive parameter search, Top-K ranking, validation-aware regime scoring, and production winner selection happen here.

Stage 3

Risk Management

Curated governance/risk policies are evaluated for winners and stored with baseline and selected policy metrics.

Live Alignment

Backtest, optimization, governance evaluation, and live execution now share the typed trade-intent exit surface. Regime TP multipliers are part of that surface. Per-regime metrics are attributed to the signal-bar regime, matching the live decision point.

How To Read The Next High-Risk JSON

What Should Change

The next generated ledger should include active workflow metadata, historical audit metrics, selected governance policy evidence, explicit no-trade slots, and current artifact versioning.

What Should Not Be Misread

Low-balance rejected trades do not automatically mean the strategy choice is wrong. They usually mean broker minimum lot, margin, or hard risk caps blocked expression of the signal.

Immediate Operator Guidance

  • Capture broker_specs.json before authoritative optimization.
  • Use a one-symbol optimization first if a live check is needed.
  • Inspect workflow metadata, historical audit, governance policy, and no-trade slots.
  • Do not add walk-forward as a gate between Stage 2 or Stage 3 and live implementation.
  • Regenerate pm_configs_high_risk.json only when ready for a long run.

Bottom Line

The PM is more coherent and live-aligned. Recent market structure drives selection, older data audits fragility, no-winner states fail closed, and risk-management behavior is attached to artifacts instead of being an invisible afterthought.
