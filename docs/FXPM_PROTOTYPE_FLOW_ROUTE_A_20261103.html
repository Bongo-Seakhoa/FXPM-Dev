FxPM prototype flow, Route A

One 28-day cycle, symbol first, M5 derived on demand, concurrent opportunity search, regime grouping inside one pass, then a narrow account-path qualification of a shortlist per cell. Loops are nested by indentation; the innermost ones run concurrently.

Where the parallelism sits

  • Symbol by timeframe jobs are independent and fan out across cores under the existing per-symbol queue bound.
  • Inside a job every params evaluation is independent, and inside an evaluation every opportunity is independent.
  • The only sequential piece left is the account path, now run for k candidates per cell instead of 300.

Where this differs from the operator's text

  • Regime is not an outer loop around strategies. It is a grouping inside one evaluation and becomes a loop only at shortlist and qualification. That is what makes four cells cost one pass.
  • Optuna lives at the params loop, one study per strategy. Numba lives at the signal-bar loop and inside the account kernel. Neither skips anything.
  • Timeframe iterations are independent. Nothing found on M5 feeds H1. The only cross-timeframe sharing is the M5 base.
  • Qualification is a second, narrow pass through the existing account kernel. What qualifies is what live executes.

Decisions and recommendations, 2026-11-03

  • directed Route A accepted by the operator; Route B closed. Shortlist size k = 5 per cell.
  • proposed Statistic: per regime, chain the opportunities non-overlapping, the trades a gated single-position taker would make, and rank by the lower confidence bound of total R on the TRAIN chain at P95. Dedupe by signal-vector sha; at most 2 of 5 per strategy family. All-opportunity expectancy is reported beside it as the masking diagnostic only.
  • proposed Acceptance before the statistic selects anything: chain trade list byte-equal to the kernel trade list on a fixture, and rank agreement with account outcomes on existing full-cell output.
  • proposed Tuning stays on the account path, one frozen evaluation per candidate and tier. Four studies per strategy, one per regime, sharing one evaluation cache; no multi-objective study.
  • proposed Route A is a new frozen process edition replacing the 300-attempt roster, not a patch.
